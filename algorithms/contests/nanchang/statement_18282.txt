Legacy Code
     Input file:             standard input
     Output file:            standard output
     Time limit:             8 seconds
     Memory limit:           1024 megabytes

You have a piece of legacy code:

                                       for t = 1 to k do
                                            ax1 ← (au1 × av1 ) mod M OD
                                            ax2 ← (au2 × av2 ) mod M OD
                                            ax3 ← (au3 × av3 ) mod M OD
                                            ..
                                             .
                                           axm ← (aum × avm ) mod M OD
                                       end for

where M OD = 998 244 353 and xi , ui , vi ∈ {1, . . . , n} for i = 1, . . . , m.
Given the initial values of the variables a1 , a2 , . . . , an , compute their final values after the loop runs k
times. You need to answer q such queries.

Input
The first line contains three integers n, m and q (1 ≤ n ≤ 200, 1 ≤ m ≤ 2 × 105 , 1 ≤ q ≤ 200).
The next m lines each contain three integers xi , ui , vi (1 ≤ xi , ui , vi ≤ n).
Each of the next q lines represents a query. Each query contains n + 1 integers:

   • the number of iterations k (1 ≤ k ≤ 106 ),

   • the initial values of the n variables a1 , a2 , . . . , an (0 ≤ ai < 998 244 353).

Output
For each query, output a single line containing n integers — the final values of a1 , a2 , . . . , an after executing
the code, each modulo 998 244 353.
